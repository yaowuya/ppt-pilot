# Synthetic Customer Interview Notes

**Synthetic acceptance fixture — not real customer research.**

- Study date: 2026-07-15
- Fictional product: Northstar Workspace
- Participants: six fictional administrators from the Q2 evaluation cohort
- Method: 30-minute semi-structured interviews conducted after day 30
- Confidentiality: internal-only fixture; do not upload or quote outside the workspace

## Thematic evidence

| Theme | Participants | Observation |
|---|---:|---|
| Setup checklist reduced ambiguity | 4 of 6 | Participants could see the next required configuration step without contacting support. |
| Live kickoff remained valuable | 2 of 6 | Participants with multi-team rollouts preferred a facilitated session over self-service alone. |
| Faster first value did not remove integration friction | 3 of 6 | Identity and data-import work remained the main implementation constraint. |

## Synthetic excerpts

> “The checklist made ownership clear, but our identity setup still needed a specialist.” — FI-02

> “We reached the first working workflow sooner; the rollout across departments still required a live kickoff.” — FI-05

## Limitations

The sample is small, self-selected, and linked to one fictional product cohort. The interviews explain possible mechanisms but do not establish that the checklist caused retention differences. Preserve the tension between self-service gains and demand for live support.
